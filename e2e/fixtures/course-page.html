Pine Valley Golf Club

Carved through coastal pines, Pine Valley pairs a championship layout with a relaxed clubhouse. Every hole frames the dunes differently, and the closing stretch plays straight into the sunset.

Course highlights

  • Par 72 championship routing
  • Practice facility with grass range
  • Caddie program from April through October

Tee times open eight days in advance. Members enjoy priority booking windows and seasonal clinics with our teaching professionals.
